# Career photography and film diary

The site presents a direct, curated portfolio: 46 career moments, comprising 24 photographs and 22 films and short video clips. The homepage highlights 12 editorially chosen moments, and the Behind the Frame archive offers visual category filters. Visitors see a concise description on every card and can expand longer captions in place.

## Source and stewardship (internal notes)
The media was selected from the owner's authorized data export. Media bytes are unchanged; older images and clips may have lower resolutions. Captions are inherited from the owner's posts where present, and not independently verified. Three images lack written captions and have neutral portfolio descriptions, without invented production names or collaborators. The complete private archive is deliberately not committed.

On the public site, do not mention the import source, ask visitors to find captions elsewhere, or present unverified production identifications as facts. Normal social profile links may remain in the social section, independently of the gallery.
